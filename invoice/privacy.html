Instant Invoice Receipt Maker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

In short: we store the business details, clients, invoices and payments you enter so the app can work and sync. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you. You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app.

This policy explains how Tefteq ("we", "us") handles information in the Instant Invoice Receipt Maker iOS app (the "App"). Questions: support@tefteq.com.

Your clients' information

When you add clients, you decide what information about them to enter and you're responsible for having the right to use it, for example to invoice them. We store and process it only on your behalf, to provide the App to you, and we never contact your clients or use their information for anything else.

Photos, files and ratings

The App only accesses your photo library when you choose a logo, and it doesn't take photos. PDFs of your invoices are created on your device; they're shared only when you choose to (for example by email, Messages or AirDrop through the iOS share sheet), and the temporary files are kept on your device. After you've been a paying subscriber for a while, the App may show Apple's standard rating prompt once; any rating or review you leave goes to Apple and is public on the App Store.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information or your clients' information.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Service providers

We use these providers to run the App. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and App Check): stores your account, business profile, clients, invoices and payments. Firebase privacy.
  • Cloudinary: hosts your company logo, if you add one. The image is available at a hard-to-guess web address so it can appear on your invoices. Cloudinary privacy.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases and subscriptions, and App Attest (used by App Check). Apple privacy.
  • Google: Sign in with Google. Google privacy.

These providers may store data on servers outside your country. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep data

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. When you delete your account, your profile, clients, invoices and payments are deleted, along with your sign-in account and the App's offline copy on your device. We don't keep any record about you after deletion. Logo files you replace, or that belong to a deleted account, are put on a removal list (which holds only the file's ID) and deleted from Cloudinary during our regular clean-up.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete your business details, clients, invoices and payments at any time in the App.
  • Delete your account in the App's Profile. This permanently deletes your account and the data above. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke the App's sign-in authorization. You can also remove it yourself in your Apple ID settings under Sign in with Apple.
  • Subscriptions are managed by Apple; deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us to use them. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the law requires a legal basis (such as the GDPR), we process your data to provide the service you asked for, and App Check data based on our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. Access rules let each signed-in user read and change only their own data. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children

Instant Invoice Receipt Maker is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the App.
