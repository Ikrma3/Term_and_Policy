What the app does

Instant Invoice Receipt Maker helps freelancers and small businesses bill their clients. You add your business details and logo once, then create invoices and estimates in seconds, record payments as they come in, and send a clean PDF to your client.

  • Invoices and estimates (quotes) with line items, discounts, taxes and due dates, in your own currency and invoice numbering.
  • Clients: save client details once and reuse them on every invoice.
  • Payments and receipts: record full or partial payments and see what's paid, due or overdue.
  • PDF, print and share: preview the invoice as a PDF, print it, or send it by email, Messages, WhatsApp or AirDrop.
  • Your branding: company logo, colours and paper size on every PDF.
  • Sync across your devices, and keep working offline.

The app is free to use for creating and managing invoices. Full-screen PDF, printing and sharing are part of an optional Pro subscription, with a free trial offered through the App Store.

Signing in and your data

You sign in with Apple or Google. With Google Sign-In, the app receives only your name, email address and profile picture (the basic openid, email and profile permissions). They're used to create your account and keep your invoices in sync, and for nothing else. We don't access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data, we don't show ads, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account and all its data at any time in the app.
