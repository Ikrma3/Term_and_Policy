US Citizenship Prep

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

In short: we keep your account details and your practice test results so you can review your tests and track your progress. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you. You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app.

This policy explains how Tefteq ("we", "us") handles information in the US Citizenship Prep iOS app (the "App"). Questions: support@tefteq.com. The App is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with USCIS or any government agency.

1. Information we collect

WhatDetailsWhy
Account Your name, email address and profile photo link from Sign in with Apple or Google, or the name and email you enter when you sign up with email and a password (with Apple's "Hide My Email" we receive a private relay address). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them. We also keep an account identifier and when the account was created. To create your account, sign you in and show your profile.

On your device A test you haven't finished yet, test results waiting to be saved when you're offline, which question sets you've already seen, and whether you've seen the intro screens. So you can pick up where you left off, don't lose results offline, and get new questions each time.

The short questions shown after you sign up (for example when your interview is) are not saved or sent anywhere. The App doesn't use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't ask for or collect information about your immigration status or case.

3. Service providers

We use these providers to run the App. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): signs you in and stores your account, test results, progress and subscription status. Firebase privacy.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases and subscriptions. Apple privacy.
  • Google: Sign in with Google. Google privacy.

These providers may store data on servers outside your country. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep data

We keep your data until you delete your account. When you delete your account, your profile, progress, subscription status and all your saved tests are permanently deleted, along with your sign-in account. Data kept on your device is removed when you delete the App.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in Profile. This permanently deletes the account data described above.
  • Subscriptions are managed by Apple; deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us to use them. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the law requires a legal basis (such as the GDPR), we process your data to provide the service you asked for.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. Access rules let each signed-in user read and change only their own account and test data. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children

The App is meant for adults preparing for the naturalization test and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the App.
