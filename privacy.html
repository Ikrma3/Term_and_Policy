SkinScan

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

In short: we collect only what SkinScan needs to work: your account, the skin profile you enter, and your scan results. Photos of labels are read on your device and are not uploaded. We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking. You can delete your account and data at any time in the app.

This policy explains how Tefteq ("we", "us") handles information in the SkinScan iOS app (the "App"). If you have questions, contact support@tefteq.com.

1. Information we collect

WhatDetailsWhy
Account Your name and email address from Sign in with Apple or Google (with Apple's "Hide My Email" we receive a private relay address), and an account identifier. To create your account and sign you in.
Skin profile Age, gender, whether you are pregnant (asked only if you select Female), skin types and skin goals. To personalize ingredient ratings and product suggestions. Pregnancy status is used only to flag ingredients commonly advised against during pregnancy.
Scan history The text recognized from a label, the ingredients matched, the result shown, the suggested products, and the date of each scan. So you can see past scans in the App.
Subscription status Your plan (weekly, monthly or yearly), its renewal or end date, and whether it is set to renew. To unlock premium features. Payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your card or payment details.
Free-scan counter A one-way scrambled code (a SHA-256 hash) made from your sign-in account identifier and email, together with the number of free scans used. It contains no readable email, name or scan content. To make sure the free scans are given once per person, even if an account is deleted and created again (fraud prevention).
On your device Small settings such as whether you have seen the introduction screens. To remember your choices.

Photos and camera

When you scan, the photo (from the camera or your photo library) is processed on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. The photo itself is not uploaded to us or stored by us; only the recognized text and the result are saved to your scan history. The App only accesses the camera or photos when you choose to scan.

Health-related information

Your skin profile, including pregnancy status, can be considered sensitive or health-related information. You provide it voluntarily to personalize results, you can change it at any time (Profile → Edit), and it is deleted with your account. We do not use it for advertising and do not share it with anyone except the service providers below, who store it on our behalf.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Service providers

We use these providers to run the App. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): stores your account, profile, scan history, subscription status and the free-scan counter. Firebase privacy.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases and subscriptions, and on-device text recognition. Apple privacy.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, and downloading the App's font (Google Fonts), which means Google receives your device's IP address when the font is fetched. Google privacy.

These providers may store data on servers outside your country. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep data

We keep your account, profile, scan history and subscription status until you delete your account. The anonymous free-scan counter is kept after account deletion, for fraud prevention as described above; it cannot be used to identify you.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your skin profile at any time in Profile → Edit.
  • Delete your account in Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, skin profile and scan history from our systems. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke the App's Apple sign-in authorization.
  • Subscriptions are managed by Apple; deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it in Profile → Cancel subscription or in your Apple ID settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to use them. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the law requires a legal basis (such as the GDPR), we process account and scan data to provide the service you asked for, your skin profile (including health-related data) based on your consent, and the free-scan counter based on our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. Access rules let each signed-in user read only their own data. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children

SkinScan is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the App.
