Cat Games

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

In short: we keep your sign-in email, your cat's name and your game scores so the app can work, plus a device record so free plays are given once per device. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

This policy explains how Tefteq ("we", "us") handles information in the Cat Games iOS app (the "App"). Questions: support@tefteq.com.

1. Information we collect

WhatDetailsWhy
Account Your email address from Sign in with Apple or Google (with Apple's "Hide My Email" we receive a private relay address), an account identifier and when the account was created. To create your account, sign you in and keep your data across devices.
Your cat's name The name you enter for your cat. To personalize the App and your scores.
Game sessions For each round: the game played (Mouse, Laser or Fish), the score, the count and speed settings, your cat's name, start and end times and how the round ended. To show your scoreboard.
Device record A random device identifier created by the App and kept in the iOS Keychain (so it survives reinstalling), which games have used their free play on this device, the sign-in account identifiers (for example google.com:… or apple.com:…) that have been used on the device, and when the record was created and updated. It does not contain your email, name or scores. So each game's free play is given once per device, including if an account is deleted and created again (fraud prevention).
Subscription status Whether you have an active subscription, its plan (monthly or yearly) and when it renews or ends. To unlock the games. Payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your card or payment details.
On your device A setting recording that you've seen the intro screens. So the intro is shown only once.

The App doesn't use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Service providers

We use these providers to run the App. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): stores your account, cat's name, game sessions, subscription status and the device record. Firebase privacy.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases and subscriptions. Apple privacy.
  • Google: Sign in with Google. Google privacy.

These providers may store data on servers outside your country. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep data

We keep your data until you delete your account. When you delete your account, your profile (email, cat's name, subscription status) and all your game sessions are deleted. The device record is kept for fraud prevention as described above, so games that already used their free play stay locked on that device. To have the device record removed as well, contact us; free plays on that device won't be given again.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Rename your cat at any time in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings. This permanently deletes the account data described above.
  • Subscriptions are managed by Apple; deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us to use them. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the law requires a legal basis (such as the GDPR), we process your data to provide the service you asked for, and the device record based on our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of free plays.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. Access rules require you to be signed in, and your profile and scores can be read and changed only from your own account. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children

Cat Games is a toy for cats, operated by their owners, and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the App.
