Cat Games

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

In short: we keep your cat's name, your game scores and (if you sign in) your email so the app can work, plus a device record that counts free plays. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; ads are personalised only if you allow it. Premium has no ads. We don't sell your data. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

This policy explains how Tefteq ("we", "us") handles information in the Cat Games iOS app (the "App"). Questions: support@tefteq.com.

2. Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner, a full-screen ad after some rounds, and optional ads you can choose to watch for an extra play. Premium subscribers don't see ads, and the ad service isn't started for them.

  • To show ads and stop fraud, AdMob receives information from your device, such as your IP address (and the approximate location it suggests), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
  • Tracking is up to you. The App asks for your permission with Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt. Only if you allow it can AdMob use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and show ads based on your activity in other apps and websites. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland the App first shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy choices in the App.
  • We don't receive your advertising identifier or AdMob's data about you, and we don't sell or rent your personal information. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Service providers

We use these providers to run the App. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): stores your account, cat's name, game sessions and the device record. Firebase privacy.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases and subscriptions. Apple privacy.
  • Google: Sign in with Google. Google privacy.
  • Google AdMob: ads in the free version, as described above. How Google uses advertising data.

These providers may store data on servers outside your country. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep data

We keep your data until you delete your account. When you delete your account, your profile (email and cat's name) and all your game sessions are deleted, and if you used Sign in with Apple we ask Apple to revoke the App's sign-in authorization. The device record is kept for fraud prevention as described above, so games that already used their free play stay locked on that device. To have the device record removed as well, contact us; free plays on that device won't be given again.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Rename your cat at any time in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings. This permanently deletes the account data described above.
  • Subscriptions are managed by Apple; deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us to use them. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the law requires a legal basis (such as the GDPR), we process your data to provide the service you asked for, and the device record based on our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of free plays.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. Access rules require you to be signed in, and your profile and scores can be read and changed only from your own account. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children

Cat Games is a toy for cats, operated by their owners, and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the App.
