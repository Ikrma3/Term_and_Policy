1. What the App does

The App turns your phone or tablet into a toy for your cat: you choose a game (Mouse, Laser or Fish), set how many creatures appear and how fast they move, and your cat chases them on the screen. It saves your cat's name and scores.

Play safely. Stay with your cat while it plays, and use the App on a stable, flat surface. Cats' claws can scratch screens, so consider a screen protector; we're not responsible for damage to your device. The laser game only shows a dot on the screen and no real laser is used. Stop playing if your cat seems stressed or over-excited, and give it a toy it can actually catch afterwards.
